## Standing rules distilled from the research
- Say "**get fitted**", never "undergo a fitting"; address readers by score
  ("if you shoot 100–110…"), not just handicap.
- "**cost**" beats "price" in queries; question-form H2s ("How much does…")
  match both Google PAA and AI-assistant phrasing.
- **Year-stamp** cost/chart content and refresh annually (2026 → 2027).
- Every article: honest-broker tone (the forum distrust of chain marketing is
  exactly why an independent directory can win), 1 CTA into /directory,
  cross-links per the checklist.
