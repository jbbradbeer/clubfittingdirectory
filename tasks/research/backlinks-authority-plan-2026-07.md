## Prioritized plays (effort → impact)

### 1. Turn the 1,043 shops into linkers — highest leverage, owned channel
Every claimed/verified shop gets a **"Featured on Club Fitting Directory" badge + copy-paste embed** linking to their listing. Bake the link-back ask into the existing claim-confirmation flow and the `fitter-outreach` email template. Even a 5% take rate = 50+ topically-perfect local links no competitor can match.
- Build: badge asset + embed snippet page; add ask to claim flow + outreach template.

### 2. Flagship data report as a citation magnet
"State of Club Fitting 2026" (original data over 1,000 shops) is exactly what golf journalists cite. Package a linkable **key-stats summary** and pitch ~20 golf outlets/bloggers: MyGolfSpy, GolfWRX, Practical Golf, Plugged In Golf, Golf Digest gear desk, r/golf. This earns the golfwrx-tier links the rival got once and let break.

### 3. Reclaim the rival's dead golfwrx link + poach their real linkers
Their golfwrx link is broken → pitch our report/directory to golfwrx for a live one. Pull FittingPros' referring domains, filter OUT the PR spam, pitch the few legit golf/editorial sites directly. (Tools: `link-prospecting` skill, `get_backlinks_profile` with a minDomainRank filter.)

### 4. Foundational niche links (easy, do once)
State golf associations, PGA sections, local chambers, city "best golf" lists, GolfWRX forums, r/golf where the directory genuinely helps.

### 5. Journalist sourcing (ongoing, low effort)
Founder answers golf-fitting queries on Qwoted / Featured / HARO-style services → earns editorial links + quotes.

### Do NOT
Buy PR-wire distribution like FittingPros. Low value, discounted by Google, mild risk.
