# Directory Competitor Analysis — Who Else Helps Golfers Find a Fitter (researched 2026-07-06)

Three research sweeps: (1) manufacturer "find a fitter" locators, (2) Google SERPs + fitting-chain
location finders, (3) rival independent directories. Companion to
`competitor-content-inventory.md` (content competitors, 2026-06-10).

---

## HEADLINE FINDINGS

1. **We have one real head-to-head competitor: FittingPros (fittingpros.com).** Launched Dec 2025
   (founder Jon Livingston). ~370 fitters in 7 states vs our 1,267 in 50 — but far deeper data per
   listing (fitting prices, launch monitor, bays, indoor/outdoor, brands, fitter classification)
   and the **same business model** (free listings + paid visibility upgrades). Already ranks **#1
   for "best club fitters in Florida"** with 2026-dated titles. The window where we were the only
   modern player closed ~7 months ago.
2. **Manufacturer locators have zero reviews, zero prices, and no indexable location pages** —
   Ping/PXG/Callaway are JS-only maps; Titleist 403-blocks bots; TaylorMade's locator redirect-loops;
   Mizuno redirects to a generic sporting-goods store finder. They own their *branded* queries
   ("ping fitting near me") but generate no long-tail city/shop SEO. That's exactly the space our
   1,267 static pages occupy.
3. **Big-city head terms are closed; small/mid markets are open.** "Club fitting near me/{major
   city}" SERPs = Google local pack + Club Champion (160+ studios, excellent city landing pages) +
   GOLFTEC (~238 centers) + True Spec (45+ NA) + Yelp's "Top 10 Best… Updated 2026" pages. But in
   Boise the SERP thinned to big-box store pages and a *directory state page ranked*
   (customclubfitters.com/Idaho). Chains cluster in top-50 metros; we cover the rest.
4. **"Best club fitters in {city/state}" is the winnable query class.** Currently held by
   2019-era GolfWRX threads, Lessons.com templates, FittingPros' 7 states, an abandoned-since-2022
   customclubfitters.com, GOLF.com's Top 50 (from 2020, ranks its own sister brand True Spec #1),
   and Golf Digest's 100 Best (biennial, no 2025-26 edition found). All beatable or lapsed.
5. **Nobody in the entire competitive set has golfer reviews or cross-brand comparison.** Every
   manufacturer tool presupposes you've chosen the brand. "Shops near you that fit Ping AND
   Titleist AND Mizuno" is unanswerable anywhere but here.

## THE COMPETITIVE MAP

### Tier 1 — direct directory competitors
| Site | Scale | Model | Status / weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FittingPros** (fittingpros.com) | 370+ fitters, 7 states (FL/GA/SC/AL/TX/AZ/CA) | Free listings + paid visibility (identical to ours) | THE threat. Deep per-listing data (price, tech, bays); #1 "best club fitters in Florida"; brand pages (/club-fitting/titleist). Young domain; manual curation limits scaling speed; rate-limits bots. |
| **Golf Fitting Finder** (golffittingfinder.com) | claims 50 states (~800–1,200 est.); TX = 43 fitters | Free listings, no visible monetization | Thin data (name/city/ZIP), anonymous operator, no trust signals. Dilutes "only directory" claims; low moat. |
| **CustomClubfitters.com** | 50 states + CA/UK, curated | Affiliate links | **Abandoned since Dec 2022** but legacy authority still ranks (its Idaho page cracked the Boise SERP). Its rankings are there to be taken. |
| **ProCustomClubFitters.com** | 3 TX listings | Amazon affiliate | Dead. Not a threat. |

### Tier 2 — editorial "best fitter" authorities
- **Golf Digest "America's 100 Best Clubfitters"** — biennial award since 2011; latest edition
  2023-24, apparently not refreshed. Winners display the badge everywhere. **The award vacuum is
  an opportunity** (see recommendations).
- **GOLF.com "Top 50 Clubfitters"** — Feb 2020, ranks sister-brand True Spec #1 (shared owner
  8AM Golf, disclosed conflict). Old, high-DA, still ranks.
- **GolfWRX / MyGolfSpy forums** — "best fitter in {city}" threads rank on exactly the queries we
  want; recurring anti-Club-Champion-upsell sentiment; strong pro-independent sentiment.

### Tier 3 — chains' own locators (rank, but self-serving)
- **Club Champion**: 160+ locations; the SEO gold standard — city pages with prices ($100–$400
  SKUs), fitter bios, tech lists, review counts, local copy. Ranked #1 in 2 of 5 test cities.
- **GOLFTEC**: ~238 US centers; /near/{city} metro pages; fitting bundled with lessons; $95 intro.
- **True Spec**: 45+ NA locations inside luxury venues; city pages rank top-3 in most metros.
- **PGA TOUR Superstore** (~47–74) & **Golf Galaxy** (~109): store pages rank in smaller markets.
- **Cool Clubs** (~19): almost no SEO footprint.
- "Club Fitters of America" — does not exist.

### Tier 4 — manufacturer & trade locators (authority, terrible UX)
| Brand | Locator | Key weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Ping | ping.com/en-us/fitting/find-a-fitter | JS-only; no reviews/prices; studio vs big-box rack indistinguishable |
| Titleist | titleist.com/locator (+2 more: Vokey, ball) | 3 fragmented locators; 403s bots; event churn |
| TaylorMade | taylormadegolf.com/locations.html | Redirect loop; app-gated (myFittingExp); best post-fit data story though |
| Callaway | /custom-fitting → generic retail locator | Weakest of big four; pushes online self-fitting |
| Mizuno | redirects to shared sporting-goods store finder | Best fitting tech (Swing DNA), worst locator — can't tell which dealers have DNA hardware |
| PXG | pxg.com/pages/locations (200+) | Best booking integration; but DTC store-finder only |
| Cobra | dealer-locator?filter=fitters | Best fitter tiering taxonomy (6 tiers); worst booking (shared email inbox) |
| Srixon/Cleveland | us.dunlopsports.com/stores | Server-rendered (rare) but mixes in tennis shops; shipped broken Maps config |
| True Temper/PX | truetemper.com/fitting | Two domains of dead legacy locator paths |
| Trackman | trackman.com/locator | Sim/range only — no fitting taxonomy |
| Foresight | **none** | "GCQuad fitting near me" intent is unowned |
| AGCP | agcpgolf.com/agcp-directory | Circa-2010 UX, no map/zip search — **partnership/data target, not competitor** |
| ICG | incg.memberclicks.net/icg-member-map | Same — credential data source for verified badges |

### Adjacent
- **Yelp** — the only horizontal directory consistently ranking (its "Top 10… Updated 2026" pages
  appeared in all 5 test-city SERPs). Real category with traction.
- **Lessons.com** — programmatic "10 Best Golf Club Fitters in {City} (2026)" pages ranked in
  Denver + Phoenix. Template worth noting.
- **CaddieHQ** — big programmatic content library on fitting questions; content competitor, not a
  directory. **GolfNow**'s fitting page is just a Club Champion/GOLFTEC ad. Thumbtack/Angi/
  Skillest/TeachMe.To: no fitting category.
- **International**: no independent fitting directory found in the UK (or, apparently, Australia)
  — open first-mover markets if we ever expand.

### Where do WE currently show up?
- #1 for our own brand name; #2 in a generic "golf club fitting directory" SERP (behind Golf
  Digest, ahead of GolfNow/FittingPros/Club Champion).
- **Not in the top-10 for any of the 5 test-city queries or "club fitting near me."** Our organic
  presence on money queries is effectively zero today.

## STRATEGIC READ

**Where we structurally win:** coverage (1,267 listings incl. the hundreds of cities every chain
skips) · neutrality (anti-upsell, pro-independent sentiment is live in forums) · reviews/ratings
(nobody has them) · cross-brand comparison (impossible on any OEM tool) · indexable pages (OEMs
ceded the entire long-tail) · existing infrastructure FittingPros lacks (provenance ledger,
verified badge, lead capture, 50-state sitemap already indexed).

**Where competitors beat us:** FittingPros' per-listing depth (prices, launch monitor, bays) ·
Club Champion's city-page quality · OEM authority on *current* certified-dealer status · PXG/
Titleist direct booking vs our lead forwarding · branded queries (unwinnable — don't fight them).

## RECOMMENDATIONS (ranked)

1. **Steal FittingPros' wedge before they scale: add `fitting price range` + `launch monitor tech`
   + `fitter classification` (independent / big-box / chain / OEM) to listings** — the facts
   ledger (listing_facts) is built for exactly this. Pricing is the #1 forum question and the
   basis of their #1 ranking.
2. **Retitle + upgrade state/city pages to the winning SERP format:** "Top Golf Club Fitters in
   {State} for 2026 | Compare Tech & Pricing" — year-stamped, comparison-framed. Start with
   states where FittingPros is absent (43 of 50) and small/mid metros the chains skip.
3. **Launch a "best fitters" editorial layer** on top of directory data — "Best Club Fitters in
   {city/state} (2026)" — to take the queries currently held by 2019 forum threads and a directory
   abandoned in 2022. Longer-term: an annual **"Best Independent Fitters by State" award** to
   inherit the lapsed Golf Digest 100 Best vacuum; the badge gives shops a reason to claim/pay.
4. **Aggregate OEM certification badges onto neutral listings** ("Ping certified", "Cobra Premier
   Fitter", "AGCP Master Clubfitter") — their authority, our UX. Approach AGCP/ICG as data/
   partnership sources.
5. **Independent-vs-chain comparison content** ("Club Champion vs an independent fitter — prices
   compared") — pairs with the June content-gap list item #3 and matches live forum sentiment.
6. **Watch FittingPros monthly** (state coverage count, new features). If they reach 50 states
   before we match their data depth, they own the highest-intent comparison shopper.
