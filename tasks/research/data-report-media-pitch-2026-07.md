**Asset to pitch:** https://clubfittingdirectory.com/guides/state-of-club-fitting-2026
**Goal:** earn genuine golf-media backlinks + citations (the authority lever). Founder sends manually, like fitter-outreach.
**Golden rule:** offer the outlet a *story with original data they can't get anywhere else*. Never ask for a link — ask them to cover/cite the data; the link follows.

## The newsworthy angles (ranked by hook strength)

1. **"America's biggest golf states are fitting deserts."** California, Texas, Florida & New York = ~⅓ of the population but only 16% of listed shops. **California ranks dead last: 1.3 shops per million residents.** Counterintuitive = most clickable.
2. **"Vermont, not Florida, is America's club-fitting capital."** 26.2 shops/million vs 3.7 national avg. Surprising underdog stat.
3. **"Indoor fitting has won."** 87% of fitting providers fit indoors (486) vs 13% outdoors (75). Trend/industry story.
4. **"TrackMan owns the fitting bay."** TrackMan in 66% of 310 tech-verified fitting shops; Foresight (GCQuad/GC3) second at 28%. Gear-nerd catnip.
5. **"Fewer than half of golf shops actually fit clubs."** 44.8% of 1,268 shops offer fitting. Consumer-useful.
6. **"What a fitting really costs."** Median entry fitting $120; 35% under $100. Cost/value angle.

Methodology line (builds trust, include when asked): *"Analyzed every one of 1,268 golf shops across all 50 states + DC in the Club Fitting Directory — services, environment, launch-monitor tech, price, and per-capita access."*

## Sending discipline (avoid the spam trap)
- Personalize the first line to each outlet (reference a recent piece of theirs). No mass BCC.
- One angle per outlet — lead with the stat that fits *their* audience (table above).
- Send 5–8/week, follow up once after ~5 days.
- Track sends/replies alongside the fitter-outreach log so nothing double-fires.
- **Do NOT** use PR-wire distribution (that's the FittingPros mistake — Google discounts it).
