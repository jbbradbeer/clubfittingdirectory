# Competitive Landscape — Club Fitting Directory Market (2026-07-26)

Source: OpenSEO MCP (keyword metrics ×16, find_serp_competitors on 12-query set,
domain overviews ×2, ranked keywords ×2, live SERPs ×4). ~1,600 credits;
balance started 7,812. Companion to `directory-competitors-2026-07.md` (07-06)
and `competitor-fittingpros-2026-07.md` (07-20).

## Market read

- **Market leaders (organic, this query set):** Golf Galaxy, Titleist, Club
  Champion, Reddit, PXG, Ping — retail chains + OEMs own the 18K/mo head terms
  ("golf club fitting near me" pos 1–7). No directory touches page 1 of head
  terms except FittingPros creeping in (pos 9–15).
- **Most winnable opportunity:** calculator/tool queries (KD 0, 3.6K+720+590+590
  = ~5.5K/mo cluster) — SERPs are full of tiny low-authority tool sites, proof
  the door is open — and "club fitting cost" (2.9K/mo, KD 3, no directory
  present, we hold real price data).
- **Biggest barrier:** authority gap. FittingPros: 1,256 keywords, ~3.9K est.
  visits/mo, climbing. Us (third-party view): **40 keywords, ~4 est. visits**,
  all long-tail shop-name crumbs at pos 12–100. Our calculators (live 6 days)
  are absent from all four calculator SERPs. AI Overviews sit at rank 1 on
  every calculator query.

## What's working (formats)

1. **City landing pages with keyword-in-path URLs** (fittingpros
   /golf-club-fitting/{city}) — carrying their head-term rankings.
2. **Free calculators feeding the directory** — their pos 3–9; micro-sites
   prove no authority needed.
3. **Individual shop pages winning shop-name navigational queries** (lenny's
   pos 5, prolink pos 4–8, david ayres pos 6) — they monetize OUR market's
   shop names.
4. **Pricing transparency pages** (Club Champion /pricing ranks for cost
   queries).
5. AI Overview rank-1 on every informational query — structured, citable
   content matters (our llms.txt/facts work aligns).
