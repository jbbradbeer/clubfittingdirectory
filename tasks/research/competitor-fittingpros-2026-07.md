## Biggest lesson
Their only true page-1 real estate is **tools**: `/golf-tools/golf-club-distance-calculator`
and `/golf-club-length-calculator` rank pos 2–11 across ~10 calculator queries
("golf club length calculator" 3.6K/mo pos 9; distance-calculator family 90–590/mo
pos 2–7). Cheap to build, ranks without authority, feeds the directory. **We have no tools.**

## Best opportunity to beat them
Their authority is **manufactured**. Backlink profile is ~90% press-release
syndication blasts (financialcontent.com, marketminute.com + dozens of identical
4-links/3-pages PR sites, spam scores 5–40) in three bursts: Nov–Dec 2025
(202 links), Feb 2026, May 2026 (122 links). Genuine editorial links: a texags
forum post, a Webflow showcase, and ONE GolfWRX link — **now broken**. A handful
of real editorial links (data-report pitch, GolfWRX presence) beats this profile
on quality; their rankings rest on sand.
