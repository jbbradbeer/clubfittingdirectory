# Voice of Customer — Reddit + Golf Forum Mining (researched 2026-06-10)

Real questions golfers ask about club fitting, in their own words (r/golf, r/golfequipment via PullPush archive; GolfWRX, MyGolfSpy, THP forums via search extraction). Use these phrasings in titles, FAQs, and article copy — they are what AI assistants get asked verbatim.

## Theme 1 — "Is fitting worth it at MY level?" (VERY HIGH frequency; the #1 question)
Posters self-identify by score ("I shoot 100-110"), not handicap; frame as cost-benefit ("is it WORTH it"). Tall golfers use height ("I'm 6'4\"") as their permission slip; "20-year-old hand-me-down clubs" is the other common frame.
- Phrasings: "Getting fitted for high HCP?" · "Is it actually going to be worth it getting fitted for new irons?" · "When should I get my golf clubs fitted?" · "I see a lot of people say getting fitted isn't worth it for beginners" · "Is club fitting overrated or is it worth the money?"
- Anxieties: "my swing is still changing — will the fitting be obsolete in 6 months?" · "I'll waste the fitter's time" · launch-monitor data is noise on mishits
- Upvoted advice an honest article must include: (184 pts) "Buy some used clubs and figure some shit out then get fitted in a year or so." · (43 pts) "Wait until you can consistently make good contact…" · BUT ALSO (10 pts) "Go to the pro. Get fitted. Do not spend $500 without doing this." · Consensus nuance: high handicappers DO benefit from basics (length, lie, flex ballpark); common threshold "worth it if you can break 100, otherwise lessons." Honest fitters who tell beginners to come back after lessons are praised.

## Theme 2 — Lessons vs fitting, which first? (HIGH)
- "Lessons or club fitting, or both? What to do first?" · "Should I invest in new clubs or coaching first?"
- Consensus: lessons first, then fit the improved swing — but a strong counter-current: badly-fitting clubs sabotage lessons. The "Phantom Miss" story: golfer spent 2 years fixing his iron swing when irons were bent 3-4° upright; after correction 7-iron speed jumped 75-80 → 85 mph.
- Article angle: honest "both, in this order, plus the cheap 30-minute basics-check an independent fitter can do."

## Theme 3 — Price anxiety + "get fitted there, buy elsewhere" (VERY HIGH)
- "Driver Fitting Cost?" · "How much should a full bag fitting cost?" · "I don't feel comfortable dropping $1500" · "Should Fittings be Free?"
- Real numbers quoted: $50 Dick's (applied to purchase) · $95-100 GolfTec · $175 Club Champion (waived w/ purchase) · free at many retailers · MyGolfSpy survey: ~65% effectively fitted free; only ~2% paid >$500
- Sticker shock: CC driver build $1,128; "4.5 hrs at Club Champion… near $5,000"; "$1,400 for JUST THE SHAFTS"
- Success story (68 pts): "$100 fitting at Second Swing → $250 used driver… $300 well spent" (gained 50-70 yds)
- (21 pts) "Get fitted first. Get told what to buy. Then go online and buy pre-owned." — extremely recurrent strategy.

## Theme 4 — Distrust of big chains (HIGH) — OUR INDEPENDENT-FITTER ANGLE
- Club Champion: "$1500 over OEM"; install fees "$620-$1,100+"; puring ($320) "gimmicky"; QC horror stories; universal adapters ≠ final club
- True Spec: "irons quoted $3,590, identical specs $1,935 elsewhere (85% markup)"; "$1,000 driver"; sales pressure
- GolfTec: pressure to purchase through them; "not all fitters are Master Club Fitters"
- Big-box: "a crapshoot in terms of fitter quality"
- BUT the chains' fitting process itself is praised (Trackman, knowledge).
- Community verdict: "Use their fitting, don't buy their builds" + "find independent fitters, such as those recommended by local head pros."

## Theme 5 — Finding/choosing a fitter (HIGH on forums; highest commercial intent) — OUR CORE FUNNEL
- "Where do you go to get fit for clubs?" · "Fitting at retail or local pro shop?" · endless geo threads ("Club fitting in the Seattle area", "Boston area club fitter") · access pain: fitters "an hour+ away", "Sub70's nearest fitter was 4 hours away" · lefty access problems recur
- What makes a GOOD fitter (community wisdom): asks lots of questions about your game · hands-on care ("almost lying down on the green to check lie angle") · depth over brand count · "research fitters beforehand rather than defaulting to the nearest shop" · quality varies fitter-to-fitter even within one company
- Red flags: brand bias, sales pressure, only trying two shafts, recommending an untested "comparable" shaft

## Theme 6 — What happens at a fitting / first-fitting nerves (MED-HIGH)
- "First fitting tomorrow, any tips on what to expect/look for?" · "don't understand what's involved" in woods/wedge/putter fittings
- Anxieties: embarrassment hitting badly in front of the fitter (universal) · not understanding the data afterward · disappointment patterns (two shafts only, untested substitutes, time-boxed)
- Green flags: fitter "explained everything… made sure I tried every club I wanted"

## Theme 7 — Indoor vs outdoor / launch monitor trust (MEDIUM)
- "Test indoors then outdoors and it doesn't work" · mat-vs-turf lie-angle horror story (fitted 2° flat on a mat → pain; re-bent 1.5° upright → 7-10 yds + consistency) · "swing speed says X-stiff but fitted into stiff" feel-vs-numbers debate
- Advice: "if you get fit indoors, go test it outdoors as well." Independents with outdoor/turf capability have an angle.

## Theme 8 — Online/remote fitting skepticism (MEDIUM, growing)
- "Takomo 101 with online fitting — good enough?" · LAB video fitting doubts ("how do they determine lie angle from video?")
- Emerging consensus: online OK for casual/15+ HCP budget buyers; serious golfers want hands-on → funnels to in-person fitters.

## Theme 9 — Fix/adjust what you own vs buy new (MEDIUM; maps to independents' repair services)
- "Should I reshaft my irons or just buy new ones?" · bending current clubs to fitted specs · "old clubs still serviceable after years away?" · grip-size debates · Ping factory restoration story · "can you get fitted INTO used clubs?" (yes — and it's the most upvoted money strategy)

## Theme 10 — Fitting by club type (MEDIUM)
- "Which fitting matters most — driver or irons?" (forum lean: irons ≥ driver) · putter fitting "even at $125 worth it for peace of mind" (SAM PuttLab praised) · wedge fitting "doesn't seem to be a thing… more feel than numbers" · "how often should I get re-fitted?"
