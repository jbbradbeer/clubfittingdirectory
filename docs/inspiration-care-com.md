# Design Inspiration: Care.com
**URL:** https://www.care.com
**Reviewed:** February 2026
**Why it's relevant:** Care.com is one of the best examples of a modern service directory on the internet. Like BTG, it connects people searching for local providers with real businesses. It's well-organised, visually engaging, and clearly converts visitors into users. We should use it as a benchmark.

---

## 1. What Care.com Is

Care.com is a directory that helps families find caregivers — babysitters, nannies, pet sitters, housekeepers, tutors, etc. People search by ZIP code, browse profiles, read reviews, and contact providers directly.

**Why it's a good benchmark for us:** It's the same core idea as BTG — someone searches for a local service provider, browses a directory of listings, and makes contact. The structure, the trust signals, and the search UX all translate directly to what we're building.

---

## 2. Homepage Layout (Top to Bottom)

### Announcement Banner (very top)
A thin coloured bar across the very top of the page with a promotional message (e.g. "Save 20% on a membership"). Simple and easy to add — great for promotions or featured shop deals later.

### Navigation Bar
- Logo on the left
- Dropdown menus in the middle: "Find care", "Find jobs", "List your business", "Resources"
- "Log in" text link + "Join now" green pill button on the right
- **Lesson for BTG:** We should add a "List your shop" option in our nav for shop owners. This opens up a monetisation path.

### Hero Section (the big top section)
- Full-width real photograph of people (warm, human, inviting)
- Bold headline: *"Connecting families with quality, local caregivers"* — simple, clear, tells you exactly what the site does in one sentence
- A single search input (ZIP code) + one green Search button
- **Lesson for BTG:** Our hero should have: a clear one-line headline ("Find the best golf club fitting shops near you"), a location/ZIP search input, and one clear button. Nothing else. Don't clutter it.

### Category Cards
6 cards in a row, each with an illustration and a label + arrow: Child care, Senior care, Adult care, Housekeeping, Pet care, Tutoring.
- **Lesson for BTG:** We should have category cards below our hero: "Club Fitting", "Custom Build", "Equipment Retail", "Demo Days", etc. This lets golfers self-select what they need immediately.

### Social Proof — Provider Showcase
A row of real provider photos with their name, specialty, star rating, and years of experience overlaid on the image. A fourth card shows sub-categories ("Activities and camps", "Daycares", "Senior living communities").
- **Lesson for BTG:** Feature 3–4 real shop photos/profiles on the homepage. Show the shop name, location, star rating, and a short descriptor ("Titleist Certified Fitter", "25 years in business"). This builds trust and makes the directory feel alive.

### Trust & Safety Section
Split layout: photo collage on the left, bullet points on the right.
- "Safety is at the heart of our community"
- Background checks required
- 24/7 safety monitoring
- Tips for safer choices
- **Lesson for BTG:** We can adapt this as a "Why trust our listings?" section. E.g. "All shops are verified", "Real Google reviews", "No fake listings". Addresses the #1 concern a first-time visitor has — can I trust this site?

### Pricing/Membership Section
Teal (blue-green) background panel. Headline: *"More than access. It's support for every step."* Four benefit tiles (Search safer, Quality care, Unlimited hires, Exclusive discounts) + "See plans & pricing" button. Phone mockup shown alongside.
- **Lesson for BTG:** This is the monetisation section. We can adapt this for "Featured Listings" — show shop owners the benefits of a paid listing (top placement, verified badge, direct contact button). The teal background makes it feel premium without being aggressive.

### Scrolling Category Ticker
A large, playful, animated row of category names scrolling across the screen in different colours and fonts ("infant care 🖼️ math tutors 🖼️ toddler wranglers…").
- **Lesson for BTG:** A scrolling ticker of golf-related search terms ("blade fitting · driver fitting · custom wedges · Titleist certified · Callaway fitting") would be visually engaging and SEO-friendly. Great for a mid-page section.

### "Find Care On Your Terms" Section
Three simple steps explained:
1. Post a job
2. Compare profiles
3. Read reviews

Clean, light background, with a "Get started" button.
- **Lesson for BTG:** A simple 3-step "How it works" section: 1. Search your location → 2. Browse shop profiles → 3. Book a fitting. Makes the site feel approachable to first-time visitors.

### Footer
Dark green background. Four columns:
- About Care.com (About us, Newsroom, Careers, Terms, Privacy)
- Get help (Safety, Articles & Guides, Help Center)
- Discover (Nanny tax help, List your business, Affiliate program)
- App store badges + Social media icons (Facebook, X, YouTube, Instagram)

**Lesson for BTG:** Our footer should have a "For Shop Owners" column — this is free marketing to the shops we want to list. Also include social links once we have them.

## 3. Individual Listing Card Design

From the app mockup shown on the site, each provider card includes:
- Round profile photo
- Name + green verified badge (checkmark)
- Location (city, state, ZIP)
- Star rating + number of reviews
- Starting price per hour
- Experience level ("3 years experience")
- Social proof tag ("Hired by 5 families in your neighbourhood")
- Heart/save icon + "Contact" button (green, pill-shaped)

**Lesson for BTG:** Our shop cards should include:
- Shop photo
- Shop name + "Verified" badge (if we add verification)
- City, State
- Google star rating + review count
- Services offered (e.g. "Club Fitting · Custom Build")
- A standout fact ("TPI Certified · 15 years in business")
- "View Shop" button

## 4. Key Design Principles to Copy

| Principle | What Care.com does | What we should do |
|---|---|---|
| **Clarity first** | One headline, one search box on the hero | Same — don't clutter the homepage |
| **Real photos** | Real people, not stock art | Use real shop photos, not generic golf images |
| **Trust signals** | Background checks, verified badges | Verified listings, Google reviews, years open |
| **Colour with purpose** | Green = trust/action, teal = premium, pink = warmth | Pick a consistent colour system for BTG |
| **Progressive disclosure** | Simple search first, detail later | Don't show all filters upfront — reveal on demand |
| **Mobile-first** | App mockups shown everywhere | Make sure our site looks great on phone |
| **"For businesses" path** | "List your business" in nav | "List your shop" for shop owners = revenue path |
| **SEO through categories** | City + category landing pages | State + service type pages (already partially built) |

## 5. Things Care.com Does That We Don't Have Yet (Future Ideas)

- **Saved/favourited listings** — heart icon to save shops for later
- **"Hired by X families nearby"** type social proof — we could show "X golfers viewed this shop this month"
- **User accounts** — golfers creating profiles, saving searches
- **Reviews written on-platform** — not just pulling from Google
- **"List your business" self-serve flow** — shop owners sign up and manage their own listing
- **App** — probably not needed early, but good to keep in mind

---

## Summary

Care.com is a masterclass in directory UX. The biggest lessons for BTG are:

1. **Keep the homepage dead simple** — search box, categories, done
2. **Show real listings on the homepage** — make it feel alive
3. **Add trust signals early** — golfers need to trust the directory
4. **Build a path for shop owners** — "List your shop" = monetisation
5. **Use real photos** — makes the site warm and credible
6. **Think mobile first** — most golfers will find us on their phone
